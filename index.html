ABOUT US

Who We Are

LifeCare Nursing & Medical Services (Pvt.) Limited is a PHC-registered home healthcare company serving patients and families since 2025. We provide professional Nursing Care at Home, elderly care, home physiotherapy, doctor home visits, and medical support through qualified healthcare professionals.

  • Our Head Office is located in Near Zee Garden Main Shekhupura / Lahore Road Faislabad, with a Branch Office in Faislabad, Shekhupura, Lahore. We also offering 24/7 nursing care, medical supervision, rehabilitation, and compassionate support for older adults and long-term care patients.

HOME CARE FACILITY

Elderly/Senior Care at Home

Our Elder Care Services and Senior Care Services at Home provide compassionate support for older adults in the comfort of their own homes. Our trained caregivers assist with personal hygiene, bathing, dressing, Diet & Nutrition, medication reminders, mobility support, companionship, dementia care, and other daily activities, helping seniors live safely, comfortably, and independently while improving their quality of life.
